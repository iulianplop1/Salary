How Projections Are Calculated

📐 Normal Day

Shift hours × 158.45 DKK/h + supplements (lager 9.65 + frost 8.55 + shift premium). Weekday shift premium: 49.10/h. Weekend: 106.65/h.

⚡ Extra Day

First 3 hours at 150% of hourly rate, remaining hours at 200%. Same supplements apply on top.

🎉 Holiday Day

Same as Extra: first 3h at 150%, rest at 200%. Supplements still apply.

🔴 Overtime Day

Normal shift pay + OT hours at 200% of hourly rate.

🟠 Sick Day

Paid at normal base rate (including standard supplements).

⚪ Vacation Day

Days not worked. Earns 0 DKK, but decreases expected hours.

🧮 Deductions

AM-bidrag (8%) → A-skat (38% with 5,652 DKK fradrag) → Pension (2%) → ATP (99 DKK) → Canteen (84 DKK).

How the SU Fribeløb Works

📏 Fribeløb

The maximum you can earn alongside SU without repaying any. Calculated on A-indkomst (Gross income minus 8% AM-bidrag). Note: The SU department does not take the 8% AM-bidrag you pay into account. It is completely ignored and does not eat into your limit!

🎓 Videregående (2026)

With SU: 20,749 DKK/month. Without SU/orlov: 23,598 DKK. Not in education: 45,420 DKK.

🏫 Ungdom (2026)

With SU: 15,297 DKK/month. Without SU/orlov: 23,598 DKK. Not in education: 45,420 DKK.

📅 Annual Calculation

The limit is calculated for the full calendar year. You can earn more in one month and less in another — only the annual total matters.

⚠️ Exceeding the Limit

If your total annual income exceeds the fribeløb, you must repay part of your SU. Check su.dk for exact repayment calculations.

💡 Tip

You can opt out of SU in high-earning months to get a higher fribeløb for that month (23,598 DKK instead of 20,749/15,297). Use the dropdown per month above.
